About & sources

An unofficial ARC Raiders companion by snowball. Track upgrade requirements and compare up to three weapon builds, including levels, attachments and durability.

Saved snapshots

374 items, 10 tracker cards, 23 weapons and 37 mods. This is not a live game feed. The structured weapon snapshot is dated April 16, 2026; other source dates are incomplete. Values may lag current patches.

Relative DPS is a comparison metric, not measured damage per second. Durability-adjusted sell value uses an approximation. Missing or unsupported comparisons remain unquantified; see the source for calculations.

Your progress

Progress and builds are saved in this browser. Nothing is synced to the game or uploaded. Use the tracker's Export/Import Progress controls to move progress between browsers or from an older local copy. Browser storage is specific to its website address.

No accounts or analytics. GitHub Pages handles hosting requests; item images load from ARC Raiders Wiki, which receives those image requests. External images can be unavailable.
